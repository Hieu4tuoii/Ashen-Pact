ASHEN PACT v2.0.0 — THIRD-PARTY SOUND EFFECTS ATTRIBUTION & LICENSES
====================================================================

All sound effects in this directory were acoustically curated, trimmed,
layered, EQ-sculpted, and mastered from permissive free sound libraries:

1. "Oculus Audio Pack 01" by Oculus VR, LLC
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)
   License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Source URL: https://developer.oculus.com/downloads/package/oculus-audio-pack-1/
   Modifications: Trimmed transient windows, resampled to 32kHz mono PCM16,
   layered with CC0 foley/magic/firearm elements, EQ-filtered, and peak-mastered.
